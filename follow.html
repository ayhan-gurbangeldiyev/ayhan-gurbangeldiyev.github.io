How to follow

  1. Choose an RSS reader. Use a web, desktop, or mobile app that supports Atom feeds.
  2. Add a feed or source. Copy the address above and paste it into your reader’s add-feed field.
  3. Read when it suits you. Your reader checks for new notes. Refresh and notification timing depend on that app.

Following by RSS uses your reader; no email address is needed here.

What you’ll receive

Full articles with their code, diagrams, references, and any AI-assistance disclosure. The feed includes up to {{ site.feed.posts_limit | default: 50 }} recent notes; the writing archive keeps the complete published list.

Original publication dates stay attached to each note. A meaningful revision can carry an updated date; a visual redesign does not turn an old note into a new article.

Seeing XML in your browser?
