## Hi, I’m Ayhan.

I’m an AI engineer working on AI agents, retrieval systems, and production software. My work spans model training, APIs, deployment, evaluation, and human review.

Engineering Notes is a place to think carefully about the systems around the model: the architecture, the tools, the evidence, and the failure modes. The central question is simple: **what makes an AI system worth trusting in practice?**

## What you’ll find here

- **Research notes** that connect primary sources to practical decisions, with their limitations intact.
- **Engineering deep dives** into architecture, evaluation, retrieval, and observability.
- **Build and failure notes** when there is actual implementation evidence to share.
- **Decision records** that explain the constraints behind a choice.

Measured results, published research, illustrative examples, and opinions should be distinguishable. Meaningful AI assistance is disclosed in the article. A promising approach is a question to investigate, not a benchmark result.

## How these notes are written

Research notes link to the original work and keep reported results attached to the tasks, models, and budgets used in that work. Build notes need inspectable implementation evidence and reproduction details. Diagrams are labeled as conceptual unless they represent actual measurements.

Publication dates record when a note first appeared. An updated date is added for a meaningful change to the argument or evidence. Corrections should explain what changed; a visual redesign does not make old research new.

If you find an error, [email me](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with the article URL and the passage or source in question. Suggested corrections are reviewed against the available evidence.
